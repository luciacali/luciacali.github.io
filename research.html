Fungal Metagenomics

My undergraduate Thesis at the University of Maine involved using environmental DNA (eDNA) to investigate mycorrhizal fungal communities and their recovery following long-term environmental change. I performed DNA extractions and PCR, maintained sample inventories, and used R to run bioinformatic sequence processing and downstream data analysis/visualization. This work contributed to research examining recovery of ectomycorrhizal fungal communities following long-term nitrogen and sulfur addition.

Atlantic Cod eRNA

My undergraduate research assistantship included work on developing environmental RNA (eRNA) approaches for detecting Atlantic cod spawning activity. My work focused on evaluating candidate mRNA biomarkers and developing an RT-qPCR assays following analysis of RNA-seq data. I designed and evaluated primer and probe candidates using NCBI Primer-BLAST,and Benchling, with the goal of developing a specific and reproducible molecular assay for detecting cod spawning activity from environmental samples. During this undergraduate assistanship, I designed and optimized RT-qPCR assays which achieved high quantitative performance (R² = 0.998)
