About

I graduated from the University of Maine in 2024 with a B.Sc. in Ecology and Environmental Sciences. While my undergraduate curriculum emphasized evolutionary biology and systems ecology, my research assistantships provided me with extensive experience in molecular biology and genetics.

I intend to continue my education with a focus on biomedical and biosecurity applications of biology. My current interests include antimicrobial resistance, neurodegenerative disease, and synthetic biology.

I am currently continuing my education independently through scientific reading, research, and writing while preparing for graduate study.
